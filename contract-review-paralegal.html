FAQ

Contract review questions

Can a paralegal review complex commercial contracts?
Paralegals can conduct first-pass review, identify non-standard clauses, flag missing provisions, and prepare structured summaries — all under attorney direction. Legal judgment and client advice remain with the attorney.
How do you handle confidential client contracts?
An NDA is signed before any documents are shared. Your paralegal operates only within the systems you authorize, and all materials remain within your control.
Can you support high-volume NDA or vendor agreement review?
Yes. Routine agreement review — NDAs, vendor contracts, standard commercial agreements — is one of the most common contract tasks we support. We can scale support to your review volume.
What does "first-pass review" mean?
First-pass review means your paralegal reads the contract, identifies key terms and clauses, flags non-standard or missing provisions, and prepares a summary for attorney review. It does not replace attorney judgment — it reduces the time attorneys spend on initial review.

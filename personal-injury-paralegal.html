FAQ

Questions about PI paralegal support

Can your paralegals handle high-volume medical record caseloads?
Yes. Medical record organization and chronology preparation is one of the most common tasks our PI paralegals support. We can handle multiple matters simultaneously under your direction.
Do your paralegals prepare demand packages?
We provide demand package support — medical summary, damages worksheets, and draft demand letter content — all under attorney direction and subject to attorney review before sending.
How is client medical information kept confidential?
An NDA is signed before any records are shared. Your paralegal operates only within the systems and access you authorize. All records remain within your control.
Can your paralegals work inside Filevine or Clio for PI cases?
Yes. Our paralegals are familiar with Filevine, Clio, MyCase, and other platforms commonly used by PI firms. We orient to your specific system before beginning work.

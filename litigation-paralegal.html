FAQ

Litigation paralegal questions

Can your paralegals handle complex multi-party litigation?
Yes. Our litigation paralegals are trained to manage document organization, discovery workflows, and case administration across complex matters. Work is always completed under attorney supervision and direction.
Can they draft motions and briefs?
Your paralegal can provide drafting support for motions, briefs, oppositions, and other litigation documents under attorney direction. All drafts are subject to attorney review before filing or use.
How do you handle privilege and confidentiality in discovery?
An NDA is signed before any case materials are shared. Privilege designations are made under attorney direction, and all work is completed within the access structure you authorize.

Our paralegals are familiar with Clio, Filevine, MyCase, PracticePanther, Smokeball, and other practice management platforms used by litigation firms. We orient to your specific system before beginning.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a remote paralegal service?
A remote paralegal service provides trained legal support professionals who work remotely inside your firm's systems — handling research, drafting, document review, discovery, and case management — under attorney supervision. No office space, no benefits, no long hiring process.
How is this different from hiring a paralegal?
Traditional hiring takes weeks or months, requires benefits, office space, and long-term commitment. Amanah provides a matched paralegal in approximately 24 hours, starting with a 15-hour free trial, with no long-term contract required to begin.
Can the paralegal work in my legal software?
Yes. Our paralegals are familiar with Clio, Filevine, MyCase, PracticePanther, Smokeball, and other practice management platforms. No new tools required.
Is confidentiality protected?
Yes. A mutual NDA is signed before your paralegal sees any client documents or matter details. Your paralegal only has access to what you explicitly authorize.
What if I want to stop or change paralegals?
During the 15-hour trial, you pay nothing if unsatisfied. After the trial, if the fit isn't right, we replace your paralegal at no additional cost. There's no long-term lock-in required to start.

Exhibit M · FAQ

Questions from attorneys

How does the 15-hour free trial work?
You give us a real task — research, document review, a deposition summary, discovery management, or another paralegal-level task. Your paralegal completes 15 hours of work. If the quality doesn't meet your standard, you pay nothing. If it does, you decide whether to continue on an ongoing basis.
Where are your paralegals located?
Our paralegals work remotely and are trained specifically on U.S. legal workflows, U.S. legal research, and U.S. practice management platforms. Amanah Legal Solutions is a U.S.-registered company.
How quickly can a paralegal start?
Approximately 24 hours from your first call to your paralegal beginning work. We handle matching, onboarding, and NDA execution so you don't have to wait weeks for support to begin.
Can I choose my paralegal?
We match your paralegal based on your practice area, software, and task requirements. If after the trial period the match isn't right, we will replace your paralegal at no additional cost.
What kind of work can they handle?
Legal research and memos, document review, discovery management, drafting support, deposition summaries, medical record summaries, contract review, case chronologies, case administration, and more. All work is completed under attorney supervision.
Can they work inside my existing legal software?
Yes. Our paralegals are familiar with Clio, Filevine, MyCase, PracticePanther, Smokeball, and other practice management platforms. Tell us what you use and we'll confirm compatibility before placement.
Do you sign an NDA?
Yes — always. A mutual non-disclosure agreement is signed before your paralegal sees any client documents, matter names, or confidential information. Confidentiality is not optional — it's step one.
How does confidentiality work?
NDA is executed first. Your paralegal is then given access only to the specific systems and matters you authorize. All work is completed within your authorized access structure and reviewed by you before use.
Can I start part-time?
Yes. After the 15-hour trial, you can continue on an hourly basis — as few or as many hours as your practice needs. We also offer monthly retained-hours packages. No minimum hours required to start.
What happens after the trial?
If the work meets your standard, you can continue on an hourly or monthly basis. We'll discuss rates and a support structure that fits your firm. There's no pressure to commit to a long-term contract.
Can you support U.S. law firms across all states?
Yes. We support U.S. law firms nationally. Our paralegals are trained on federal procedure and common state-specific practice requirements. Jurisdiction-specific research and local rules compliance are confirmed with you during onboarding.
Can I request a practice-area specialist?
Yes. When you contact us, tell us your primary practice area — litigation, personal injury, immigration, contracts, or general legal support. We match specifically, not generically.
What if the paralegal isn't the right fit?
During the 15-hour trial: you pay nothing if unsatisfied. After the trial: if your paralegal isn't performing to your standard, we will replace them at no additional cost. We stand behind the placement.
How does billing work?
The 15-hour trial is free — no payment required upfront. After the trial, billing is discussed on your strategy call. We offer hourly billing and monthly retained-hours packages. Exact rates are confirmed before you commit to anything.

FAQ

Legal research questions

What databases do your paralegals use for research?
Research tools and database access are discussed during onboarding. We work within the research platforms your firm uses or has access to. Your paralegal does not independently subscribe to research databases on your behalf.
How is the research delivered?
Research is delivered in a structured memo format — issue, relevant authority, organized analysis, and citations — ready for attorney review. We adapt the format to your firm's preferences.
Can the paralegal conduct research in multiple practice areas?
Yes, with attorney direction. We match paralegals based on your primary practice area, but legal research can span multiple subject areas as directed by the attorney.
How do I review the research before it's used?
All research memos are delivered to you for review before any use. Nothing is sent to clients or filed without attorney review and sign-off.

FAQ

Discovery support questions

Can your paralegals handle multi-party discovery across several matters?
Yes. Managing discovery across multiple matters simultaneously is one of the core tasks our litigation paralegals support. We maintain master tracking logs and coordinate deadlines across matters under your direction.
How are privilege designations handled?
Privilege designations are made under attorney direction. Your paralegal identifies potentially privileged documents and flags them for attorney review — the attorney makes all privilege calls.
Can the paralegal draft discovery requests and responses?
Yes — under attorney direction. Drafts are delivered for attorney review, revision, and approval before service on any party. Nothing is sent without attorney sign-off.
How is confidentiality protected during document review?
An NDA is signed before any case documents are shared. Your paralegal operates only within the systems and matter access you authorize.

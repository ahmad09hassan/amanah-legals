FAQ

Trial questions

Is there really no credit card or commitment required?
Correct. The 15-hour trial requires no credit card, no contract, and no commitment. After the trial, you decide whether to continue. If you choose not to, you owe nothing.
What if I only have small tasks — can I still use the trial?
Yes. Many attorneys start the trial with a handful of smaller tasks that collectively take 10–15 hours. The goal is to test whether delegation works in your practice — not to have one enormous task.
How is confidentiality protected during the trial?
An NDA is signed before any case documents are shared — including during the trial. The same confidentiality protections that apply to ongoing clients apply from day one.
What practice areas are supported?
We currently support litigation, personal injury, contract review, immigration, and general civil practice. If you're unsure whether your work fits, the free strategy call is the right first step.
What happens after the 15 hours?
We follow up to hear how the trial went. If you'd like to continue, we'll match you with a paid plan based on your volume. If not, that's it — no invoice, no awkward conversation.
